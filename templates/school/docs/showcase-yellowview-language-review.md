# Yellow View language content: for a fluent speaker to review

`test/fixtures/showcase-yellowview/` holds six world-language files: `spanish-i-grade-9`, `spanish-ii-grade-10`,
`spanish-iii-grade-11`, `spanish-iv-grade-12`, `french-i-grade-9` and `french-ii-grade-10`. They were written by a model
and pass the structural check (`pnpm yellowview:check-content`), but nobody fluent has read them. Before the school is
shown to a language teacher, a Spanish speaker and a French speaker should read these files and correct what is wrong.

Lesson notes, mark-scheme points and the teacher's feedback are in English. Prompts, readings, cards, model answers and
learners' answers are in the target language, with English where a beginner needs it (Spanish I and II, French I and II
glossed more than III and IV).

## What to read for

- Accents, inverted punctuation and elision (Spanish: accents, the opening question and exclamation marks; French: accents,
  the cedilla, apostrophes in je/j', l'/le, liaison notes in readings).
- Agreement of gender and number in the model answers and in the examples in the readings.
- Verb forms in the model answers, above all: reflexives (Spanish II, French II), preterite against imperfect (Spanish III),
  the present subjunctive (Spanish IV), the passé composé with avoir and with être (French II).
- Whether each cultural statement is accurate (holidays, meals, regions) and not a stereotype.
- Register: tú and usted, tu and vous, used as the lesson says they are.
- That each reading gives only the vocabulary the course level has met.

## Mistakes that are meant to be there

Learners' answers contain the genuine slips a student at that level makes, and the feedback corrects them. Do not
"fix" these in the answers; check that the feedback's correction is right:

- Spanish (seen in Spanish I and IV): `Me gusta los deportes` for `me gustan`, `yo gusto los gatos` for `me gustan`,
  `quien` for `que` with a thing, `quien trabajan` for `que trabaja`.
- French and the rest: the files were not read line by line for this note, so the slips in French and in Spanish II and III
  are not listed here. The reviewer should list them as they find them.
- Weak answers in every language file are sometimes written entirely in English, on purpose, and the feedback asks for
  the target language.

## Not claimed

No part of these files has been checked by a fluent speaker. Treat every example sentence as unverified until then.
