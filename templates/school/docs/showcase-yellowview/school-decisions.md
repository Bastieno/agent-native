## Left unset, and why

- **Assessment styles:** the app ships no US assessment style, so questions are worded plainly, which check-school-setup reports
- **Block or A/B scheduling:** the timetable repeats every week, so a school on alternating days or a block schedule cannot be shown; Yellow View runs seven periods a day
- **A grading scale per grade:** the app holds one scale per school, which suits a US high school
- **Counselors:** the app has no counselor role, so counselors are not on the staff list
- **Quarters 2 to 4:** dates only; nothing is taught in them
- **Late hand-ins:** the app records only handed in or not, so lateness is not modelled; work not handed in counts as zero
- **Year-group struggling lists:** the app answers "who is struggling?" per class only; the year-group lists here are the learners flagged in at least a third of their classes
- **Attendance:** the app has no attendance feature, so report cards carry none
