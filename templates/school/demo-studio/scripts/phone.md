# Phone video script (draft 1)

About 70 seconds, shown on a phone. Same format as grant.md: plain paragraphs are spoken,
[brackets] are on screen, {braces} are delivery tags.

---

## 1. Ask

[Principal, on a phone. Tap the chat button, ask "What needs my attention this week?"]

What needs your attention at school this week? Just ask.

[The answer, live: 21 classes with lesson notes not marked ready, nothing blocking.]

Twenty-one classes have lesson notes written that nobody has marked ready. And nothing is blocking.

## 2. The timetable

[Open the menu, Timetable. Tap Wed, then Fri.]

The timetable is on the phone too, one day at a time. Built from the school's own day, and accepted by a person.

## 3. Marking

[Teacher's home: 5 papers to mark.]

Now I'm a teacher. A quiz was handed in this week, and none of it is marked. I ask Ellai to mark it.

[The answer, live: five papers marked, who did not hand in.]

Five papers marked, with each score and grade, and a list of who didn't hand in.

It asks me before it publishes anything.

[Close.]

That's Ellai. A school you run by asking.
